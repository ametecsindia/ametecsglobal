Full description
SmartPRS is the mobile app for companies that run HR, attendance and payroll on SmartPRS.

Employees:
• Punch IN / OUT — at the office by QR, or from the field within your geo-fence
• See today's status and your last 7 days: present, late, leave, weekly off and holidays
• Apply for leave and see your leave balance
• Request an attendance correction
• Download your payslips and letters
• Get alerts for approvals and announcements

Managers and HR:
• Approve leave and attendance corrections on the go
• See who is in today and who is missing
• The same menus and permissions as the SmartPRS web application

Secure by design: every phone is registered and approved by your company's administrator, and a lost phone can be revoked at any time.

New to SmartPRS? Tap Explore Demo to try a sample company with ready-made data.
